Where to invest in South Africa

Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA)
R36 000/yr limit. Growth and withdrawals are 100% tax-free. Best first account to open.
Retirement Annuity (RA)
Contributions are tax-deductible (up to 27.5% of income). Access only from age 55.
JSE Index ETFs
Low-cost funds tracking the JSE Top 40 or S&P 500. Satrix, CoreShares, and 10X are popular providers.
Money market fund
Low-risk, liquid. Ideal for your emergency fund. Returns ~7–9% in SA currently.

Debt payoff strategy

Avalanche method: Pay minimums on all debts, then throw extra money at the highest-interest debt first. Saves the most money.
Snowball method: Pay off the smallest debt first for quick wins and motivation, then roll that payment to the next debt.
Rule of thumb: Any debt above 10% interest is an emergency. Clear it before investing.

Key money habits

Pay yourself first: Set up a debit order on payday to move savings before you can spend them.
Emergency fund first: Build 3–6 months of expenses before investing. Keeps you from selling investments in a crisis.
Max your TFSA annually: R36 000/year, completely tax-free. After 10 years this is transformative.
Avoid lifestyle creep: When you get a raise, save at least half the increase instead of spending it all.
Track every rand: People who track spending save on average 20% more than those who don't.
